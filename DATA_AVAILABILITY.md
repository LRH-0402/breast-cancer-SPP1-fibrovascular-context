# Data availability

All analyzed inputs are publicly available from their original repositories.

- GSE176078, GSE161529, GSE246613, and GSE114725: NCBI Gene Expression Omnibus.
- Wu breast cancer Visium data: Zenodo record 4739739.
- Independent eight-section spatial data: Figshare item 21591429.
- TNBC spatial and clinical data: Zenodo record 14204217.

Exact access URLs, roles, audit states, and sample counts are listed in `data_manifest.tsv`. `provenance/raw_data_sha256.tsv` records the filenames, sizes, and SHA-256 values used in the completed analysis. Raw matrices and images are not redistributed because they remain available from the source repositories and substantially increase archive size.

The included result tables and metadata are derived exclusively from these public resources. No controlled-access individual-level genomic data are included.
